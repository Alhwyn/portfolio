Realizing that tech events in Victoria were majority for adults, my friend Kai Prairie and I started Scrapyard Victoria - a hackathon specifically designed for high school students.

We successfully raised over $2000 CAD to make this event happen through our amazing sponsors: [VIATEC](https://viatec.ca), [HackClub](https://hackclub.com), [Tiny](https://tiny.com), **Shreena Kalaria**, and [TenFold Victoria](https://www.tenfoldvictoria.com/) funded prizes and served as our advisors throughout the planning process.
