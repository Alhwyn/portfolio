PhotoBomb is a multiplayer photo party game for 3 to 12 players. Similar to Cards Against Humanity, each round we randomly choose a prompt from a selection of 1 to 7 prompts, and players take turns sharing photos that best match the prompt. The group votes on the funniest or most creative submission to decide the winner.

Visit [photobomb.online](https://photobomb.online) to check it out.
