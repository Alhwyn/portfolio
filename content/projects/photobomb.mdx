PhotoBomb is a mobile party game that lets friends challenge each other to take themed photos in real time. The app was inspired by classic party games and built to encourage creativity and laughter.

My main focus was on seamless user experience, fast photo uploads, and a fun, vibrant interface. The project taught me a lot about mobile development, real-time data, and the importance of user feedback in shaping a product.
